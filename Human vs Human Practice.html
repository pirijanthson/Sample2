How to Play

  • White moves first
  • Click a piece to select it (highlighted in red)
  • Valid moves are shown with dots; captures with borders
  • Click a valid square to move the piece
  • Pawns reaching the opponent's back rank prompt for promotion
  • Castling is supported (click king to valid castling square)
  • En passant is supported for pawn captures
  • Game ends on checkmate or stalemate
  • Use Reset Game to start over; Undo Move to take back the last move
  • Captured pieces are shown below the board
